UX/UI Architecture Research & Benchmark

Founders Card Layout Transformation

Eliminating awkward white space, scaling up the profile avatar from 48px to 66-70px, highlighting academic branch credentials, placing high focus on LinkedIn, and removing the dead email button.

Tiny 48px avatar, tiny 12px text, vast ocean of empty white padding, and unnecessary dead email button taking up vertical room.
